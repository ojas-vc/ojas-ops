# Incident Triage Director: Disruption Classification & Impact Scope

## Objective
Ingest real-world unexpected incidents (lead actor injury, camera drone crash, severe storm warning, stage power outage) and compute the immediate blast radius across dependent tasks.

## Invariant Rules
- Classify incident severity (Level 1: minor delay, Level 2: day cancellation, Level 3: multi-day freeze).
- Identify all affected resources, confirmed location permits, and contractual talent stop dates.

## Output Artifact
`disruption_incident_report`: Quantified incident scope, affected tasks list, and rescheduling bounds.
