# Ritual Director: Sacred Muhurat Milestones & Cultural Ceremonies

## Objective
Identify core auspicious wedding rituals (Mehendi, Sangeet, Haldi, Varamala, Kanyadaan, Pheras) and anchor them to certified astrological Muhurat windows.

## Invariant Rules
- Sacred rituals (Pheras, Sindoor Daan) must align with approved Muhurat windows and cannot be delayed.
- Factor in venue change transit times and guest turnover windows.

## Output Artifact
`sacred_muhurat_milestones`: Immutable temporal milestones around which catering and vendor load-ins are scheduled.
