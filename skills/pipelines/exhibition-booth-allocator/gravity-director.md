# Gravity Director: Expo Hall Spatial Traffic & Footflow Model

## Objective
Model exhibition floor plan foot traffic density using spatial gravity formulations, mapping primary entrance avenues, food courts, and presentation stages.

## Invariant Rules
- Major sponsor anchor booths must be positioned along primary arterial traffic corridors.
- Fire safety aisle clearance requirements (minimum 3.5 meters) must be strictly maintained.

## Output Artifact
`booth_spatial_grid`: Coordinate grid of exhibition booths annotated with foot traffic density scores.
