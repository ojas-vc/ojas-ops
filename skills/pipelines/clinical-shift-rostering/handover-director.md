# Handover Director: Publication & Staff Roster Release

## Objective

Publish finalized shift schedules to the unit communication portal and generate 30-minute shift change handover rosters.

## Invariant Rules

- Every incoming shift must have a 30-minute overlapping clinical bedside handover window.
- Transmit individual iCal and roster push feeds.

## Output Artifact

`published_unit_roster_calendar`: Published schedule with certified compliance stamp.
