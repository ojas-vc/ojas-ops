# Fatigue Equity Director: Fatigue Audit & Gini Equity Gate

## Objective

Audit the candidate roster against union fatigue policies, calculate weekend rotation fairness (Gini index), and present to the Chief Nursing Officer for sign-off.

## Invariant Rules

- Flag any rotational whip (e.g. Day shift immediately following Night shift within 24h).
- Ensure weekend shift assignments do not exceed a Gini inequality coefficient of 0.18.
- Require formal human sign-off from Nurse Leadership.

## Output Artifact

`fatigue_compliance_audit`: Quantitative violation audit and equity score breakdown.
