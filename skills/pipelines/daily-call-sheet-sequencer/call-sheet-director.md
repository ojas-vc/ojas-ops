# Daily Call Sheet Director: Intra-day Shot Sequencing

## Objective

Sequence camera setups to minimize camera axis turnarounds ($\Delta \theta \le 45^\circ$), align exterior lighting with sun azimuth/golden hour, and enforce 6-hour meal penalty intervals.

## Invariant Rules

- Hard call: First meal break within 6 hours of general crew call.
- Mandatory 12-hour turnaround between wrap and next call.
- Require 1st AD formal sign-off before releasing call sheet to cast & crew.
