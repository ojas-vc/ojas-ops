# Leave Director: Festival Holiday Demand & Manning Thresholds

## Objective
Collect employee regional festival leave requests (Diwali, Eid, Lunar New Year, Christmas) and establish minimum safe staffing operational thresholds per department.

## Invariant Rules
- Critical life safety, security, and utility departments must never drop below certified emergency manning baselines.
- Prioritize seniority and rotational historical fairness when approving holiday leave slots.

## Output Artifact
`festival_leave_requests`: Validated pool of leave applications mapped against minimum station coverage constraints.
